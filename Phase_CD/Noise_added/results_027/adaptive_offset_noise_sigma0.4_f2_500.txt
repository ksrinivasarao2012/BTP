[INTERPRETATION - offset]
  THE STEALTH/HARM BIND (the clean demonstration). phantom_center_offset = metres from the
  hugged real obstacle's CENTRE. offset~0 => phantom ON the real obstacle: blocks ~no new
  space (harm~0) AND zero offset => temporal can't/needn't catch it (harmless). As offset
  grows => harm rises (protrudes into the corridor) AND recall rises (caught). BIND holds
  if harm and recall climb TOGETHER and temporal>=off at every offset -> no free lunch.
