[INTERPRETATION] bias=0 (every sigma) is the paper's unbiased default. As bias grows, IF the
  zero-mean assumption breaks we expect PRECISION to fall (honest neighbours flagged) and NO-HARM
  to go negative. If no-harm stays ~0, the own-sensing cushion absorbs the false flags (limitation
  bounded). Watch the sigma x bias interaction: at low sigma the single-frame tolerance is tight,
  so bias should bite harder; at high sigma the wide tolerance may mask it. QUANTIFIES assumption (vi).
